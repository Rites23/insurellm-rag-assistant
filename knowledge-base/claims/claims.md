# Insurellm Claims Process

Customers can submit insurance claims through the claims department.

Claims process:

1. Customer submits claim details.
2. Supporting documents are reviewed.
3. The claim is assessed.
4. Approval or rejection decision is provided.

Required claim information:

- Customer name and contact details
- Policy number
- Description of the claim
- Supporting documents related to the claim
- Any relevant evidence or reports

Claims should contain accurate customer information.

Customers can contact Insurellm support for assistance with claims.
